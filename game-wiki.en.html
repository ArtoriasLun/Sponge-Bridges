Survivor's Dossier: City G

You're Sam, a courier. The day the red fog rose past the town limits, City G became a dead zone. You wake up strapped into a cargo frame, and soon a cargo tower stacks up on your back — every item you carry counts, in real weight and real volume.

No safehouses, no waypoints. Hunger, thirst, and fatigue are real numbers, and the scent and sound you leave behind get tracked by tar men for real.

Combat & Armor

Hit → armor → weak point: three layers of resolution, one after another.

Armor Resolution

A hit first checks whether your gear covers the struck location; only if it's covered does the material resistance table decide how much damage gets through — the whole suit rolls as one unit, not piece by piece.

Weak Point System

Normal spot
Weak spot

Hitting a weak point breaks armor more easily, triggers criticals, and sometimes carries a control effect. Take the Tar Hulk: a headshot noticeably cracks its armor faster, and an eye shot lands almost like it's wearing nothing — it all comes down to aim and positioning; stacking stats won't help you land hits consistently.

Red Fog Damage

Red fog damage resolves through the normal armor pipeline too — it's not an "ignore defense" effect that skips your gear. Wearing the right material genuinely mitigates part of it.

How Monsters Find You

Monsters don't know your coordinates — they find you by scent and sound.

Scent Tracking

Every step you take leaves scent, which then spreads and fades over time. Monsters with tracking ability (like the Tar Hulk) climb up the gradient toward stronger scent; taking a detour, opening distance, or slowing down all let it fade and break.

Noise Tracking

Gunfire, footsteps, doors, reloading, alarms — nearly every action makes noise. When something is heard but not seen, the monster only marks a rough direction, a puzzled guess, not a beeline to your real position. Explosions are the one exception — they currently make no sound at all.

Corpse Revival

A freshly downed corpse almost never gets back up — the longer it sits, the higher the odds climb. Keep burning through a corpse that was destined to revive, and it comes back at full health as something worse — this doesn't touch anything still alive and fighting; it only decides what the corpse turns into.

Mood & Attitude

Every monster carries an anger value and a morale value. Getting hurt, hearing noise, a companion dying — triggers like these push it between hostile / following / ignoring / fleeing. Feeding it can lower anger enough to make it ignore you, but any attack on it re-triggers full aggression instantly.

The Town of City G

The town regenerates every run — streets, buildings, and supplies all differ, but a handful of landmarks always stay put.

Tutorial Apartment Tower

The one fixed, hand-authored four-floor tutorial tower — the same content every run.

4F
Wake-up floor. Alun lies badly hurt at the stairwell — saving him is your call.
3F
Guarded by Building Security. Teaches encounters and looting corpses.
2F
Trap floor. Spike boards and caltrops mixed in.
1F
Security Captain guards the front gate. Everything from the three floors above comes together here.

The tower isn't a one-path building, and the key isn't the only route through — how you get downstairs is yours to figure out.

Hidden Basement

The entrance is hidden inside the tutorial tower — a chain that teaches feeding and rewards exploration; finding your way through it is on you.

Police Station

A terminal that takes real skill to crack, entirely optional — skip it and it changes nothing about the run.

Hack Difficulty

If your electronics skill isn't sharp enough, you might not crack this terminal at all — good or bad, the outcome is yours to find out by trying.

On this page Red Fog & Tar Men· Key Characters

Red Fog & Tar Men

The red fog is the true shape of the quarantine ring outside town — a wall of mist condensed from alien tar seeping up from the ground, sharing one source with the tar men.

People touched and soaked by the red fog get remade into tar men. All three are nodes on the same storyline, not separate settings bolted together.

Key Characters

Bode

A low-key civilian hero at the edge of City G's dead zone, dressed in colors that pop against the wasteland palette, easygoing to the point of indifference.

Alun

Bode's (self-declared) apprentice, a cybernetically augmented survivor badly hurt after taking on a Tar Hulk alone.
