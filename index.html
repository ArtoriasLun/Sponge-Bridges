G City

You are Sam, a courier. The day the fog rose past the edge of town, it became a danger zone — the roads are still there, the houses are still there, it's just everyone in them turned to tar. You still run supplies for the people who are left; soon there's a tower stacked higher with every haul — supplies, spare parts, your last ration — the weight and the bulk both count, for real.

Hunger, thirst, and fatigue are real needs. Scent and noise really do give you away. Cross the streets and find your own way out — or find the one person still willing to reach back.
