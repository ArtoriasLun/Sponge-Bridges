G-City

You are Sam (or whatever you prefer to call him). Ever since the toxic Red Mist seeped in from the outskirts, G-City has fallen. You must stack supplies tall upon your back, evade the Tarred, search for clues to clear the mist once and for all, and... try not to starve to death mid-delivery.
